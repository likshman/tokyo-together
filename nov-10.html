Option A — Narita Express: simpler transfer

Narita Airport Terminal 2·3 → Shinjuku → Daitabashi → WANOYADO

  1. Follow JR / Narita Express signs to the Terminal 2 railway level using lifts.
  2. Buy five complete one-way tickets to Shinjuku, including reserved seats. Ask for four nearby seats and the nearest fifth in the same carriage. Use the issued tickets as instructed rather than paying the base fare twice with an IC card.
  3. Board the service/carriage specifically continuing to Shinjuku, allowing 85–105 minutes riding. Not every train section goes there.
  4. Allow 25–35 minutes at Shinjuku with luggage. Follow Keio Line signs; ask staff for the lift route to a local stopping at Daitabashi.
  5. Take a suitable direct local/all-stations train: Shinjuku KO01 → Sasazuka KO04 → Daitabashi KO05, approximately 7–10 minutes riding, plus waiting. Avoid an express that skips Daitabashi.
  6. Use the lift to street level and walk slowly to the house, 7–10 minutes plus station-exit time.

Published ordinary Narita Express fare checked during planning: JPY 3,330 per adult. Total allowance including Keio: JPY 17,000–18,000 for five, subject to travel-date fares. JR East fares/tickets

Choose this when parents are tired, road traffic is poor, or fewer transfers/luggage handling matter most. If the next direct train is full, wait for a service with five seats rather than rushing through extra changes. Allow 2.5–3.5 hours after lunch to reach WANOYADO, including ticketing/waits, lifts, and Keio transfer. For a meal finishing about 16:30–17:15, the conservative house-arrival envelope is about 19:00–20:45, later with disruption; actual service choice may be faster.
