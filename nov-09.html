Supper among the lanterns

Liuhe Night Market is just a few minutes on foot. Start at the Zhongshan 1st Road/Liuhe 2nd Road entrance, browse a short stretch, and come back the same way — a compact evening, not a cross-city outing.

Meal

≈20:00–21:15

Liuhe Night Market / 六合夜市

Find a seated main dish first — seafood congee, noodles or dumplings — then share a snack and a drink while you take photos of the lit food street. The market runs daily, Mondays included; individual stalls vary.

  • Budget about TWD 300–500 each (TWD 900–1,500 for three) — an allowance, not a menu quote.
  • Carry small Taiwan cash and check prices before ordering.
  • Seats aren’t reserved; if none are free, use a nearby seated eatery. Keep standing queues under 15 minutes.
