FRI13NOV Pamanhikan & Getting to Know Each OtherDay 5 of 12 · Akihabara → Tsukiji → Shimbashi → Akihabara → Lumiere

Tsukiji, Tamiya and Electric Town after dark

A market lunch, Papa’s flagship visit and an evening in Akihabara

Leave WANOYADO at 09:15. Store five cases at Akihabara, explore Tsukiji Outer Market over lunch, then head to Shimbashi for Tamiya and a 17:00 yakitori dinner. If you finish early and want more time in Electric Town, explore Akihabara first and have dinner there instead.

Return to Electric Town for Radio Kaikan and the lights. Finish sightseeing at 19:35, collect the cases by 20:00 and aim for Lumiere at 20:50–21:15.

Plat Tsukiji and the market lanes

Pick up the current map at 4-16-2 Tsukiji. Browse a short loop of cooking tools, tea and dried goods; this is the Outer Market, not a wholesale auction.
