Make the morning easy

Buy tonight. Breakfast at home tomorrow.

The owner suggests picking up breakfast at Keio Store the evening before. It opens at 10:00. Bring chilled food home promptly and refrigerate it.

On November 10, use the existing grocery stop. On other evenings, buy only if it fits the return home.
